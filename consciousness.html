THE INQUIRY

Different ways of knowing.

Neuroscience, contemplative traditions, and lived experience each describe consciousness differently. I’m interested in what happens when they are allowed to inform one another rather than compete.

I don’t think the mystery needs to be solved to be worth living inside. I’m drawn to questions that stay open: what is attention, what is a self, and what changes when we look closely.
