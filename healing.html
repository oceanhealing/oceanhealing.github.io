HEALING & EMBODIMENT

Come back
to the body.

Somatic presence, mindfulness, and meditation: a way back into the body.

AN INVITATION

If you’ve been feeling dull, dimmed, or stuck…

Those feelings are not a failure. They are clues.

When life gets hard, it is not only the hardship that wears us down. It is also the quiet voice that says, I am alone.

Healing, as I understand it, is a process of remembering, resourcing, and reconnecting with the body and with each other. Not fixing what is broken, but making room for what is already trying to emerge.

WHAT TO EXPECT

A gentle, grounded process.

FIRST

Arrive

We begin by slowing down and noticing what is here: sensation, breath, mood, without needing to change anything.

THEN

Explore

With curiosity and care, we follow what the body and nervous system are communicating, at a pace that feels safe.

FINALLY

Integrate

We gather what emerged and find small, practical ways to carry it back into daily life.

SOMATIC PRESENCE

Presence begins in the body.

Presence is not a state to achieve. It is a sensation to notice.

Somatic practice means listening through the body: the weight of the feet, the rhythm of the breath, the quiet signals of the nervous system. Mindfulness and meditation are ways of returning to that listening, again and again, with kindness.

THREE DOORWAYS

Ways I practice.

BODY

Somatic presence

Noticing sensation, posture, and the nervous system, and letting the body set the pace.

ATTENTION

Mindfulness

Meeting each moment as it is, with curiosity rather than judgment, in daily life as much as on the cushion.

STILLNESS

Meditation

Sitting, breathing, and resting in awareness, in simple forms anyone can begin with.
