Healing doesn't always mean becoming someone new.

Sometimes it means slowly discovering the parts of ourselves we learned to hide, the feelings we never had space to express, and the stories we have carried for longer than we realize.

For a long time, I thought healing meant getting better — becoming stronger, finding answers, and eventually reaching a place where nothing could hurt me in quite the same way.

But perhaps healing is not about becoming untouched by life.

Perhaps it is about learning to meet life differently.

To pause instead of running away. To listen instead of immediately trying to fix. To recognize our patterns without turning them into reasons to judge ourselves.

Some things need understanding. Some need time. Some need to be felt, expressed, or simply witnessed.

And not everything needs to be solved at once.

I am beginning to see healing less as a destination and more as a relationship with ourselves — one that grows through curiosity, compassion, honesty, and the courage to stay present.

Perhaps we are not trying to become someone else.

Perhaps we are making space to become more fully ourselves.
