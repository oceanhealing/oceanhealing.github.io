RELATIONAL INTELLIGENCE

The intelligence
between us.

Presence, vulnerability, and connection.

PRESENCE

We heal in relationship.

So much of what hurts us happened between people, and so much of what heals us does too.

Relational intelligence is the ability to stay present with another person, and with ourselves, through discomfort, tenderness, and difference. It can be learned, and it can be practiced.

PRACTICES

Small ways to begin.

Listen fully

Let someone finish. Notice the urge to fix or reply, and let it pass.

Name what’s true

Say what you feel in one honest sentence, without blame.

Stay a little longer

When it gets uncomfortable, take one more breath before you respond.
