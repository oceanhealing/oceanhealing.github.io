WHY I’M LEARNING

A scientific mind, an open question.

Psychedelic research is reshaping how we think about the mind, and it is still early.

I’m interested in altered states, the research on psychedelic-assisted therapy, and the cultural and ethical questions that come with it. I approach all of it as a learner, not an authority.

This page shares what I’m reading and reflecting on. It does not recommend or encourage the use of any substance.

SAFETY & HUMILITY

Please read this.

Psychedelic substances carry real risks, including psychological risks and interactions with medications and certain health conditions. Their legal status varies widely by place.

Nothing on this site is medical, legal, or clinical advice. If you are considering this path, speak with a qualified healthcare professional and learn the laws where you live.
