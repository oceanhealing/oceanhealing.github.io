CONSCIOUSNESS

What is it like
to be aware?

Curiosity about awareness, meaning, presence, and the mystery of being human.

THE INQUIRY

Different ways of knowing.

Neuroscience, contemplative traditions, and lived experience each describe consciousness differently. I’m interested in what happens when they are allowed to inform one another rather than compete.

I don’t think the mystery needs to be solved to be worth living inside. I’m drawn to questions that stay open: what is attention, what is a self, and what changes when we look closely.

“
Awareness is not something we get.
It is where we already are.

PRACTICES

Ways I explore.

Meditation

Sitting quietly with experience as it is, and noticing how awareness itself behaves.

Embodiment

Letting sensation, movement, and breath become a source of understanding.

Inquiry

Asking simple questions slowly: What is here? Who is noticing?
