Waves of emotion are consistently in and through our body. These are healing waves from the past, the time you got hurt, 
the moment you were confused what you might have done wrong. These are waves waiting to be born, to librate. This is the past
finding its way to the present, yes to this moment where you eyes are reading this while your body is experiencing worlds of words from 
the past, the past of you. Just dance with them, yes, wake up and move whichever way your body is asking you. No description, no choreography, 
just a pure reflection of what you are into a sea of movements.
